## About BuzzBundle

If you handle social activity across several networks and want a tidier way to plan posts, follow conversations, and reply to customers, BuzzBundle was made for that job. Scheduling, monitoring, and inbox management live together, so everyday promotion and interaction happen inside one desktop application.

The tool suits both teams and individuals working alone who want a structured routine for brand visibility, watching competitors, and responding to an audience. AI integration, multilingual support, and a responsive interface are there to cut down repetitive work while keeping campaign management within reach on Windows.

## Usage

BuzzBundle follows a straightforward social management flow:

1. Add or connect the platforms you plan to manage.
2. Write posts and schedule them for later publishing.
3. Review and answer messages through the unified inbox.
4. Keep an eye on brand mentions, competitor activity, and campaign trends.
5. Check analytics to refine timing, messaging, and engagement strategy.

For AI-assisted work, enable the available OpenAI or Claude API option in the application settings, then use the scheduling or workflow tools to help with content preparation and response handling.
